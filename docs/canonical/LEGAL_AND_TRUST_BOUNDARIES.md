# Legal & Trust Boundaries

TEN is not a government authority.

Never claim final visa/residence eligibility, recognition outcome, personalised tax/legal/insurance suitability, guaranteed jobs, guaranteed visas or unverified partnerships.

Initial forms must not collect passport/ID images, residence-permit images, health information, banking credentials, tax identifiers or unnecessary financial documents.

For referrals: verify provider, disclose commercial relationship, obtain consent before sharing personal data, review periodically.
