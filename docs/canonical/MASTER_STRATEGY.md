# Master Strategy

THE EXPAT NETWORK is an independent Germany pathway-navigation and decision-support ecosystem.

The market gap is not lack of Germany information. The gap is orchestration:
**Which source applies to me, what should I do first, what next, what can I assess myself, what requires an official decision, and when do I need a qualified specialist?**

- `.org` owns the journey.
- `.de` owns the tool.
- official bodies own official decisions.
- qualified specialists own regulated/expert fulfilment.
- LinkedIn builds authority and institutional relationships.
- Instagram creates discovery and human trust.

North-star metric: **meaningful next-step completions**.

No-go: job marketplace, ATS, social network, auto-apply, generic visa portal, fake partner claims, mass dashboards, content farm.
