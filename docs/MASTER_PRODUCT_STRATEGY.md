# The Expat Network — Master Product Strategy

## Mission

Help international talent relocate, integrate, and build careers in Europe while helping employers understand, attract, evaluate, and support international candidates.

## Core Business Model

The primary business is not software.

The primary business is:

1. Talent placement support
2. Out-of-EU relocation counselling
3. Visa and job-offer review services
4. Recruiter and employer consulting
5. Relocation partner referrals

Software tools exist to generate trust, traffic, leads, and qualification signals.

## Customer Segments

### Segment A — International Talent

Examples:

- India
- Pakistan
- Bangladesh
- Nepal
- Philippines
- Nigeria
- South Africa
- Latin America

Needs:

- Job opportunities
- Salary understanding
- Visa understanding
- CV improvement
- Relocation guidance

### Segment B — Employers

Examples:

- SMEs
- mid-sized companies
- scaleups
- funded startups

Approximate size:

50–500 employees

Needs:

- hiring difficult-to-fill talent
- understanding immigration realities
- candidate readiness
- retention support

### Segment C — Recruiters

Needs:

- international candidate pipelines
- relocation support
- candidate qualification support

## Revenue Priorities

### Tier 1

High priority:

- relocation counselling
- job-offer review
- salary review
- CV review
- LinkedIn review
- readiness assessments

### Tier 2

Medium priority:

- talent placement support
- recruiter support
- employer consulting

### Tier 3

Long-term:

- employer partnerships
- sponsored recruitment campaigns
- premium talent sourcing

## Product Philosophy

Tools are acquisition assets.

Tools are not standalone SaaS businesses.

Every tool must connect to at least one of:

- a service
- a lead capture flow
- a consultation flow
- a placement flow

## Geographic Focus

Primary:

- Germany
- Netherlands
- Belgium
- Austria
- Ireland

Secondary:

- Nordics
- France

## Employer Focus

Prioritize:

- SMEs
- mid-sized firms
- scaleups
- funded startups

Avoid enterprise-first workflows.

## Success Metric

Not pageviews.

Not tool usage.

Primary metric:

Qualified relocation and placement leads generated.
